Botermi - Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 10, 2025

1. General Information

This privacy policy applies to the Botermi mobile application to protect your personal information and ensure privacy. We inform you about how we collect, use, and protect your information.

2. Information We Collect

2.1. Personal Information:

  • Phone Number: For registration and authentication
  • Name and Surname: For profile completion
  • Email Address: For communication (optional)
  • Location Data: To identify service locations

3. Permissions and Their Usage

3.1. Camera Permission (CAMERA)

Purpose: QR code scanning and photo capture

  • Scan QR codes to get product information
  • Take and upload photos
  • Scan product images

3.2. Location Permission (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION)

Purpose: Google Maps services and service location display

  • Find nearest service locations
  • Map services
  • Location-based recommendations

3.3. SMS Permissions (RECEIVE_SMS, READ_SMS)

Purpose: Automatic OTP code reading and input filling

  • Automatically read OTP codes received via SMS
  • Automatically fill OTP codes into input fields
  • Improve user experience

4. How We Use Your Information

We use your information for the following purposes:

  • Provide application services
  • User authentication
  • Customer service and support
  • Improve application performance
  • Ensure security

5. Data Protection

We take the following measures to protect your personal information:

  • Data encryption
  • Secure servers
  • Limited access to sensitive information
  • Regular security audits

6. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used in the application:

  • Google Maps: Map services
  • Expo: Application development platform
  • Firebase: Backend services

7. User Rights

You have the following rights:

  • View your information
  • Edit your information
  • Delete your information
  • Restrict use of your information

8. Changes

We may change this privacy policy at any time. We will notify you of any changes.
